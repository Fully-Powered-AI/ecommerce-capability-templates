# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published inventory methods shaped it. From the small-business inventory planning method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes reading a recent week's sales beside four weeks', looking into the cause when the two differ by half or more, dating the stock-out on the faster of the two while sizing the order on the four-week rate, leaving sold-out days and one-off bulk orders out of the rate, giving no rate to an item with too little history, marking items that will run out before an order can arrive, a default seven-day buffer, counting stock already on order, rounding to the supplier's pack, keeping items with no sales off every order and showing the cash in them, an overstock limit reported as the units and cash above it, asking the owner for any seasonal change and showing the order with and without it, checking the data and the age of the stock count before any arithmetic, a summary that leads with the total to order and the count of items about to run out, one purchase order per supplier priced at the last known cost with a delivery date from the lead time, treating a supplier's minimum as the owner's decision with the extra pack shown in units, cost and days of stock, and holding each order for approval. From the inventory optimization method in kishorkukreja/awesome-supply-chain (MIT) it takes the reorder point as expected demand over the lead time plus safety stock, a reorder point raised to cover the review period as well, the stock position as stock on hand plus stock on order, and ordering up to a target when a regular review finds the position at or below the point. Fully Powered AI designed the weekly exports with set column headers that stop on a mismatch, the two-part safety stock, the target of the reorder point plus a set number of days of cover, the use of the review-period point as the line for an order at the next review, the 180-day default overstock limit, stock on order counted toward the stock-out date only when it arrives first, the pack additions shown for the supplier's other items as well as the ones on the order, and the draft purchase order with its approval line. No third-party text is reused.
