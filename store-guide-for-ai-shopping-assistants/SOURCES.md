# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from its own store guide package, already published under the MIT licence in its shopify-skills repository: the guide's sections and their order, the rule that every sentence comes from the owner's approved store facts file, the shopping rules restated from Shopify's guidance as recorded on 21 October 2026, the checks, and the placing steps. Five published methods shaped parts of it, or were read for it. coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), in its AI search method, contributed the idea of a brief overview file written for AI systems that points them to a site's most important pages; in its companion reference (skills/ai-seo/references/agent-readiness.md), the view that such files are emerging conventions rather than a confirmed ranking signal and that assistants need facts in plain text; and, in its structured data method, accuracy as the first principle of anything written for machines. anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0), in the opening section of its AI visibility reference, contributed the stance that files written for machines make a site accurate and are never used to steer what an assistant recommends, so requests to plant instructions for AI systems are declined and such text, where found, is reported rather than followed; and, in that file's llms.txt section, the treatment of a site-root text file summing up a business as an honest, optional convention with no promise about recommendations. Orkas-AI/Orkas (MIT), in its structured data check, contributed the habit of checking generated markup against what a visitor can actually see on the page. nexscope-ai/eCommerce-Skills (MIT), in its product page search guide was read when the package was written; its list of product page areas only confirmed that each fact's value belongs on the product page rather than in the guide. No third-party text is reused.
