# Fully Powered Workflow Templates: E-commerce and Retail

A store that every shopper, and every shopper's AI assistant, can understand. These workflow templates are written for the people who run an online store. They prepare the facts, the product-page reviews and the visibility checks, and the store's people decide what changes.

This repository holds 10 workflow templates for the people who run an online store. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The workflow templates

- **[AI visibility audit for an online store](ai-visibility-audit-for-an-online-store/)**: A store owner or marketer gets a dated audit of how the store reads to AI assistants and shopping agents, from its own saved public pages: eighteen fixed checks with evidence, a fix list in priority order, and a record of what the assistants actually said. Nothing on the store is changed.
- **[Chargeback response pack](chargeback-response-pack/)**: When a card dispute lands, the store owner or finance lead gets one pack: the dispute matched to its order, the evidence its reason calls for marked found or missing, a response drafted well inside the deadline, and the store's dispute ratio for the month. A person decides whether to contest and submits it.
- **[Inventory reorder plan](inventory-reorder-plan/)**: The store owner or buyer gets one plan from the latest sales and stock exports: each item's sales rate, the day it could run out, its reorder point and safety stock, what to order, what is not moving, and a draft purchase order per supplier. Nothing is ordered until a person approves.
- **[Payout reconciliation for an online store](payout-reconciliation-for-an-online-store/)**: Each month the bookkeeper gets every payout from the store's processors and marketplaces taken apart and tied to the bank: the orders, refunds, chargebacks and fees inside it, each fee checked against the agreed rate, every difference listed with its evidence. A person posts the entries and raises any dispute.
- **[Product landed cost worksheet](product-landed-cost-worksheet/)**: After an import shipment lands, the owner or bookkeeper gets one worksheet: freight, duty, brokerage, insurance and inbound fees from the invoices spread over each product by a stated method, the landed unit cost beside the cost the store records, and the margin left. A person updates any cost.
- **[Product-page facts gap review](product-page-facts-gap-review/)**: Product by product, your merchandiser sees what a shopper's AI assistant needs from each page and does not get: facts the description leaves out, images with no alt text, buyer questions left unanswered. Each gap comes with a sourced draft to fill it, waiting for the owner's approval.
- **[Slow-moving stock and markdown plan](slow-moving-stock-and-markdown-plan/)**: Before a seasonal reset, the merchandiser or store owner gets one plan from the sales and stock exports: each item's sell-through and weeks of cover, the cash tied up in dead and slow stock, and a markdown plan in dated steps with the margin each leaves and a date to stop. The owner decides every price.
- **[Stock count reconciliation](stock-count-reconciliation/)**: After a physical count, the bookkeeper and the warehouse or store lead get every item counted beside the quantity and value in the books, each difference in units and money, what to check for it, and the items to recount. Nothing is adjusted until a person approves.
- **[Store facts file](store-facts-file/)**: The person who runs an online store builds the one file every other store workflow template reads first: what the store sells, the facts each product page should state, the statements it stands behind, buyers' questions, policies, roles and access. Nothing on the store is changed.
- **[Store guide for AI shopping assistants](store-guide-for-ai-shopping-assistants/)**: The person who runs an online store gets the plain-language guide a shopper's AI assistant reads about the store: what it sells, for whom, which product line suits which need, and where the policies are. Every line traces to the approved store facts file, and a person places it.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

To take the whole collection, press **Use this template** at the top of this page: it copies every folder into a repository in your own account, yours to change.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The workflow template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
